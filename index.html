About

What is Ikhlas?

Ikhlas Institution is a Muslim Students Institution, established in 2014 E.C., dedicated to nurturing faith, knowledge, brotherhood and service.

Ikhlas Institution is a Muslim student-led initiative dedicated to nurturing Islamic values, strengthening brotherhood, and encouraging meaningful contributions among Muslim students. The institution brings students together through organised sectors, each with a distinct purpose and areas of responsibility. Its work is structured around five sectors: Da'wah, Social, Khidmiya, Academy, and Finance, working towards a shared vision of student development and collective service.

Ikhlas is organised into sectors, each with its own purpose and areas of work. Five public sectors are presented here: Da'wah, Social, Khidmiya, Academy and Finance.

Ikhlas means sincerity. The name is a reminder that every effort in the institution should be made sincerely, for the sake of ALLAH.
